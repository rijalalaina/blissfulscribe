FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is BlissfulScribe a subscription? +
No — BlissfulScribe is a one-time purchase. You pay once and own your licence forever. There are no monthly fees, no annual renewals, and no surprise charges. Your licence includes lifetime updates to the version you purchased.
Does BlissfulScribe require an internet connection? +
No internet connection is required for transcription. The Whisper AI model runs entirely on your Mac. The only time a network connection is used is during the initial app download and licence activation.
Which versions of macOS are supported? +
BlissfulScribe requires macOS 14.4 Sonoma or later. It is compatible with macOS 14 Sonoma and macOS 15 Sequoia, and will be updated to support future macOS releases.
What languages does BlissfulScribe support? +
Whisper AI supports 99 languages, and BlissfulScribe inherits this capability. Transcription quality is highest for English, but many other languages — including Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Mandarin — are also very well supported.
Can I try BlissfulScribe before buying? +
Yes. Every download includes 20 free transcriptions with full access to all features — no credit card required. Once you've used your 20 free transcriptions, purchase a licence to keep going.
What is the refund policy? +
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If BlissfulScribe doesn't meet your needs within 30 days of purchase, email us at contact@blissfulplan.com and we'll issue a full refund promptly.
