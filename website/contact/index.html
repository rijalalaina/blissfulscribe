Quick Answers

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to respond to all emails within one business day (Monday to Friday, London time). During peak periods it may occasionally take up to two business days. Pro licence holders receive priority email support.

How do I request a refund?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Email contact@blissfulplan.com with your order reference (or the email address used at checkout) and we'll process your refund promptly. See our Terms of Service for full details.

What information should I include for technical support?

To help us diagnose issues quickly, please include: your macOS version, the version of BlissfulScribe you're running (visible in the menu bar or About screen), a description of the issue, and any relevant steps to reproduce it. Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful if the problem is visual.
